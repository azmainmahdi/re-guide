# Working from cafés (sample guide, made up)

## Welcome (3 min)
Note: Have the recorder ready but not running.
Say: Thanks for joining. This takes about forty minutes, and you can stop or skip a question at any time.
Say: There are no right or wrong answers. I'm interested in how you actually work, not how you think you should.
Say: I'd like to record the audio so I don't have to take notes the whole time. Is that OK?
Note: Start the recorder only after a clear yes.

## Getting started (6 min)
Ask: How often do you work from cafés?
Ask: What made you start?
Probe: What was work like before that?
Note: Skip the probe if they already described it.
Ask: Was it your choice, or did something push you there?

## Choosing a place (8 min)
Ask: How do you pick a café?
Probe: What would make you stop going?
Probe: Do you go back to the same one, or move around?
Ask: Tell me about a café you tried once and never went back to.

## A typical day there (10 min)
Ask: Walk me through a normal day at the café, from when you arrive.
Probe: When do you do your hardest work?
Ask: Do you talk to anyone while you're there?
Probe: Staff, other customers, people you know?
Note: If they mention headphones, ask what the headphones are for.
Ask: What do you buy, and when?

## Leaving (6 min)
Ask: How do you decide when to leave?
Probe: Has anyone ever made you feel it was time to go?
Ask: Do you ever feel you owe the café something?

## Wrap-up (4 min)
Ask: Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?
Say: Thank you. I'll stop the recording now.
Say: I'll send you a short summary when the study ends.
